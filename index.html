Large-scale mapping

Projects measured in square kilometres.

Large areas need flight blocks planned to the terrain, consistent ground control and processing that holds up at scale. Every job is quoted with four figures, so you know exactly what you are buying.

Area
In km², from the site boundary you send
Ground resolution
In cm per pixel, set by what you need to measure
Coordinate system
An EPSG code, such as EPSG:32644
Accuracy
Checkpoints and a written report

Deliverables

Name the output. We deliver exactly that.

Every delivery comes with its coordinate system and an accuracy check.

DeliverableWhat it isUsed for
Orthomosaic
A georeferenced, distortion-corrected image of the whole site.
Base maps, measurement, planning.
DEM, DSM and DTM
Elevation models of the surface, and of the bare ground without trees and buildings.
Drainage, slope, earthwork, flood analysis.
Classified point cloud
3D points sorted into ground, vegetation, buildings and other classes.
Terrain extraction, vegetation height, as-built checks.
Contour map
Lines of equal elevation drawn from the elevation model.
Design, grading, planning.
Volume report

Billing, progress tracking.

From flight plan to finished map

How a project runs.

  1. 01

    Plan

    We agree the area, ground resolution, deliverables and deadline, then plan flight blocks and ground control.

  2. 02

    Capture

    Drone flights with photogrammetry or lidar sensors, tied to ground control and checkpoints.

  3. 03

    Process

    Alignment, classification and modelling into orthomosaics, elevation models and point clouds.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Files in the agreed coordinate system, with an accuracy report you can check.

Environmental and climate solutions

Measure the land. Understand the change.

The same drone data answers environmental questions. We measure what changed, show the method and the dates, and let the evidence make the case.

  • Baseline and change-detection maps
  • Flood and drainage mapping
  • Shoreline and wetland monitoring
  • Vegetation and canopy mapping
  • Site monitoring for environmental compliance

Each result states the quantity measured, the method and the date range.

About

Named for a seed that travels.

A samara is the winged seed of the maple. It spins like a helicopter blade and travels far from the tree on the wind. We work the same way: we fly, capture, and the result travels to wherever the decision is made.
